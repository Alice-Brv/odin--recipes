TIRAMISU'

The classic no-bake Italian dessert — layers of espresso-soaked ladyfingers and a rich mascarpone cream, dusted with cocoa.

INGREDIENTS
  • 500 grams mascarpone cheese, at room temperature
  • 5 large eggs, separated
  • 100 grams granulated sugar
  • 0.3 teaspoons fine salt
  • 500 milliliters strong brewed espresso, cooled
  • 2 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional)
  • 300 grams ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi)
  • 3 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting
  • 30 grams dark chocolate, finely grated (optional)

STEPS
  1. Whip the egg yolks: In a large bowl, whisk the 5 large eggs, separated egg yolks with half of the 100 grams granulated sugar until pale, thick, and doubled in volume.
  2. Fold in the mascarpone: Add the 500 grams mascarpone cheese, at room temperature to the whipped yolks and gently fold together with a spatula until smooth and well combined. Set aside.
  3. Whip the egg whites: In a clean, dry bowl, whisk the egg whites with the 0.3 teaspoons fine salt until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining sugar and continue whisking until stiff, glossy peaks form.
  4. Fold whites into the cream: Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the mascarpone mixture in two or three additions, being careful to keep as much air in the mixture as possible.
  5. Prepare the coffee dip: In a shallow dish, combine the cooled 500 milliliters strong brewed espresso, cooled and 2 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional) if using.
  6. Dip and layer the ladyfingers: Quickly dip each 300 grams ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi) into the coffee mixture for about 1 second per side — they should be moistened but not soggy. Arrange a layer in the bottom of a 9x13-inch (23x33cm) dish.
  7. Layer the cream: Spread half of the mascarpone cream evenly over the ladyfingers. Repeat with a second layer of dipped ladyfingers, then top with the remaining cream, smoothing the surface.
  8. Chill: Cover and refrigerate to allow the layers to set and the flavors to meld.
  9. Dust and serve: Just before serving, dust generously with 3 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting and sprinkle with 30 grams dark chocolate, finely grated (optional) if using.

NOTES

This recipe uses raw eggs, which carry a small risk of foodborne illness — use pasteurized eggs if you're concerned, especially when serving to pregnant women, young children, the elderly, or anyone with a compromised immune system. Tiramisù genuinely improves with time, so make it at least a few hours ahead — overnight is even better, as it allows the flavors to meld and the layers to set. Dust with cocoa just before serving, as it can absorb moisture and lose its look if added too early.
