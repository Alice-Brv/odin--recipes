POLPETTE AL SUGO

Italian Meatballs in Tomato Sauce (Polpette al Sugo) Tender Italian meatballs simmered slowly in a rich tomato sauce until juicy and infused with flavor — classic comfort food, perfect with crusty bread or pasta.

INGREDIENTS
  • 500 grams ground beef
  • 200 grams ground pork
  • 100 grams stale bread, crusts removed
  • 120 milliliters whole milk (for soaking bread)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 60 grams grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 teaspoons salt and pepper for meatballs
  • 0.3 teaspoons fresh nutmeg, grated
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced (for sauce)
  • 700 milliliters canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 fresh basil leaves
  • 0.8 teaspoons salt and pepper for sauce
  • 1 pinch of sugar (optional, to balance acidity)

STEPS
  1. Soak the bread: Tear the 100 grams stale bread, crusts removed into small pieces and soak in the 120 milliliters whole milk (for soaking bread) for about 5 minutes until softened. Squeeze out excess milk and set aside.
  2. Mix the meatball mixture: In a large bowl, combine the 500 grams ground beef, 200 grams ground pork, soaked bread, 2 large eggs, 60 grams grated Parmesan cheese, 2 garlic cloves, minced, 3 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped, 1 teaspoons salt and pepper for meatballs, and 0.3 teaspoons fresh nutmeg, grated. Mix gently with your hands until just combined — avoid overmixing, which can make the meatballs tough.
  3. Shape the meatballs: With slightly damp hands, roll the mixture into meatballs about the size of a golf ball (around 40g each). Place on a tray and refrigerate while you start the sauce.
  4. Cook the onion: Heat the 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil in a large, deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add 1 small onion, finely chopped and cook until soft and translucent, about 4-5 minutes.
  5. Start the tomato sauce: Add 1 garlic clove, minced (for sauce) and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in the 700 milliliters canned crushed tomatoes, 1 fresh basil leaves, 0.8 teaspoons salt and pepper for sauce, and 1 pinch of sugar (optional, to balance acidity) if using. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Simmer the meatballs in sauce: Carefully lower the chilled meatballs into the simmering sauce in a single layer. Cover partially and simmer gently, spooning sauce over the meatballs occasionally and turning them once halfway through, until cooked through and tender.
  7. Finish and serve: Taste the sauce and adjust seasoning if needed. Remove the basil leaves before serving. Serve the meatballs hot, spooned generously with sauce.

NOTES

Simmering the meatballs directly in the sauce rather than frying them first keeps them tender and lets them absorb flavor from the tomatoes, though you can sear them first in a little oil for extra color if you prefer. Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and the flavor deepens overnight. Serve with crusty bread, over pasta, or with a side of creamy polenta.
